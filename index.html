What is Monte Carlo?

Real trading never follows a smooth line. Even a winning strategy has streaks of wins and streaks of losses. This tab plays out your strategy 1,000 times, each with its own random order of wins and losses, then shows the spread of results: an unlucky run, a typical run and a lucky run, plus how deep the dips get along the way.

1 in 10 simulated runs ended at or below this. A realistic bad stretch for this strategy, not the absolute worst case.

The longest run of losing trades in a row in a typical run. Expect to sit through a streak like this at some point.

Bad-Case Drawdown
